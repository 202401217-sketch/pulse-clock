Add Pulse to your iPhone

1. Open this page in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow ↑).
3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Pulse then opens full screen from its own icon. To hear full Spotify songs, log in once inside the Spotify player in the app.
